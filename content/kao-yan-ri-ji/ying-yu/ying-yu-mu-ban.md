# 考研英语二图表作文替换模板

**使用顺序：选图表类型 → 填数据 → 选一组原因 → 配对应结尾。**

模板采用“三段八句”结构。各表编号相互对应，可以直接替换。替换后，按照题干检查总词数。

## 一、主模板：动态图版

适用于有年份变化、需要描述上升或下降的图表。

| 段落 | 句式功能与语法亮点 | 必背英文主干 | 【空位】直接替换指南 | 中文对照助记 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 第一段第1句：介绍图表 | 准确交代主题和时间范围 | The 【①图表】 illustrates changes in 【②指标主题】 between 【③起始年】 and 【④结束年】. | ①填：**bar chart / line graph / pie chart / table**。<br>②填名词短语，如 **consumers’ preferred shopping methods**。<br>③④填年份，如 **2015 / 2025**。 | 该图展示了【主题】在【起始年】至【结束年】之间的变化。 |
| 第一段第2句：突出主要数据 | **What主语从句＋that表语从句** | What stands out is that 【⑤主要数据完整句】. | ⑤填“主语＋变化＋数据”。<br>例如：**the percentage of online shoppers rose from 25% to 60%**。<br>其他表达见“数据描述表”。 | 最突出的是，【某项指标发生了怎样的变化】。 |
| 第一段第3句：补充对比数据 | 时间衔接，形成比较 | Over the same period, 【⑥对比数据完整句】. | ⑥填另一指标在同一时期的变化。<br>例如：**the figure for shopping in physical stores fell from 75% to 40%**。 | 在同一时期，【另一项指标发生了怎样的变化】。 |
| 第二段第4句：引出原因 | **may表示审慎推测** | Several factors may help explain this pattern. | **本句可以固定背诵。**<br>用于引出后面的两条原因。 | 几个因素可能有助于解释这一现象。 |
| 第二段第5句：原因一 | **that表语从句**；素材可带分词结构 | One possible explanation is that 【⑦原因一完整句】. | ⑦从“原因素材表”选择。<br>必须是有主语、有谓语的完整句，不能只填 **economic development** 等名词短语。 | 一种可能的解释是，【原因一】。 |
| 第二段第6句：原因二 | 递进衔接；素材可带分词结构 | In addition, 【⑧原因二完整句】. | ⑧选择与⑦同一主题、但解释角度不同的另一条原因。<br>例如：第一条写节省金钱，第二条写节省时间。 | 此外，【原因二】。 |
| 第三段第7句：归纳意义 | **过去分词短语引出综合判断** | Taken together, these findings highlight the importance of 【⑨核心需求或价值】. | ⑨填名词或动名词短语。<br>例如：**convenient and affordable shopping services**。<br>可使用“结尾替换表”。 | 综合来看，这些发现凸显了【某种需求或价值】的重要性。 |
| 第三段第8句：提出具体建议 | **it形式主语＋不定式＋so that目的状语从句** | It would therefore be useful for 【⑩行动主体】 to 【⑪具体措施】, so that 【⑫预期效果完整句】. | ⑩填谁来做，如 **online retailers**。<br>⑪填动词原形开头的措施，如 **improve product quality**。<br>⑫填完整句，如 **consumers can shop with greater confidence**。 | 因此，【某个主体】可以【采取措施】，从而【实现具体效果】。 |

### 完整背诵骨架

The 【①图表】 illustrates changes in 【②指标主题】 between 【③起始年】 and 【④结束年】. What stands out is that 【⑤主要数据完整句】. Over the same period, 【⑥对比数据完整句】.

Several factors may help explain this pattern. One possible explanation is that 【⑦原因一完整句】. In addition, 【⑧原因二完整句】.

Taken together, these findings highlight the importance of 【⑨核心需求或价值】. It would therefore be useful for 【⑩行动主体】 to 【⑪具体措施】, so that 【⑫预期效果完整句】.

## 二、静态图替换表

**只替换第一段，后两段仍按主题选择。**

| 图表类型 | 第一句：介绍主题 | 第二句：突出主要数据 | 第三句：补充比较 | 替换要点 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **调查选择型**<br>例如：阅读原因、择业考虑、休闲偏好 | The 【图表】 presents the results of a survey on 【调查主题】 among 【调查对象】. | What stands out is that 【选项A】 was selected by 【X%】 of respondents, compared with 【Y%】 for 【选项B】. | Meanwhile, the figure for 【选项C】 stood at 【Z%】. | 调查主题：**reasons for reading / preferred leisure activities / factors influencing job choices**。<br>调查对象：**college students / local residents / consumers / parents**。<br>**多选调查也可以使用这一版。** |
| **总量构成型**<br>例如：家庭支出、市场份额 | The 【图表】 shows the distribution of 【总量】 across 【类别范围】. | What stands out is that 【A】 accounted for 【X%】 of the total, making it the largest category. | By comparison, the shares of 【B】 and 【C】 stood at 【Y%】 and 【Z%】, respectively. | 总量：**household expenditure**。<br>类别范围：**different spending categories**。<br>A/B/C：**food / housing / education**。<br>第二句的A必须确实是最大项。 |

## 三、数据描述表

**直接填入⑤、⑥。放在模板句子内部时，注意首字母通常用小写。**

| 需要描述的情况 | 直接替换的英文内容 | 中文含义与使用条件 |
|---|---|---|
| 普通上升 | the figure for 【A】 rose from 【数据1】 to 【数据2】 | A从数据1上升到数据2。 |
| 明显上升 | the figure for 【A】 increased substantially from 【数据1】 to 【数据2】 | A明显增长；涨幅确实较大时使用 **substantially**。 |
| 普通下降 | the figure for 【A】 fell from 【数据1】 to 【数据2】 | A从数据1下降到数据2。 |
| 基本稳定 | the figure for 【A】 remained relatively stable at around 【数据】 | A在一段时间内基本稳定在某水平。 |
| 两项都上升 | the figures for 【A】 and 【B】 both increased, reaching 【数据A】 and 【数据B】, respectively | A、B均上升，分别达到两个数值。 |
| 两项接近 | 【A】 and 【B】 recorded similar figures, at 【数据A】 and 【数据B】, respectively | 两项数据相近，不强行写显著差距。 |
| 两倍关系 | the figure for 【A】 was twice that for 【B】 | A的数据是B的两倍，必须与实际数据一致。 |
| 人数变化 | the number of 【复数名词】 increased from 【人数1】 to 【人数2】 | 描述具体人数或数量，例如 **visitors / users / students**。 |
| 比例变化 | the percentage of 【群体】 who 【行为】 rose from 【比例1】 to 【比例2】 | 例如：**the percentage of consumers who shopped online rose from 25% to 60%**。 |

## 四、原因素材表

**同一行的两条原因，分别填入⑦、⑧。**

- ⑦接在 **One possible explanation is that** 后面。
- ⑧接在 **In addition,** 后面。
- 选择能够解释图中具体行为的素材，不按关键词机械套用。

| 主题及适用范围 | ⑦ 原因一：直接填在 that 后面 | ⑧ 原因二：直接填在 In addition, 后面 | 中文助记 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **线上购物、消费便利**<br>适合网购增长或购物动机调查 | online platforms allow consumers to compare a wide range of products and prices easily, helping them make better use of limited household budgets | home delivery saves shoppers the time and effort of visiting physical stores, making online shopping particularly attractive to working adults and others with busy schedules | **价格比较→节省预算**；<br>**送货上门→节省时间**。 |
| **数字阅读、自主学习、技能提升** | digital resources give people easy access to a wide range of learning materials, allowing them to study whenever and wherever it is convenient | developing practical skills can improve career prospects and help people deal with new challenges, giving them a clear reason to continue learning | **资源易获取→学习方便**；<br>**提升技能→改善发展机会**。 |
| **体育锻炼、健康生活**<br>适合运动参与、健康或减压动机 | regular exercise can help people manage stress and improve their physical health, making it a valuable part of daily life | nearby parks and sports facilities provide convenient places to exercise, allowing residents to stay active without spending much time or money on travel | **运动→减压和健康**；<br>**设施便利→容易参与**。 |
| **旅游、休闲、文化体验** | convenient transport connections can reduce the time and effort required for travel, making distant destinations more accessible to ordinary families | travel offers a break from daily routines and an opportunity to experience different cultures, making it attractive to people seeking relaxation and new experiences | **交通便利→出行容易**；<br>**旅游体验→放松和增长见闻**。 |
| **活动参与不足或下降**<br>适合讨论时间、费用等障碍 | heavy study or work commitments leave people with limited free time, making it difficult for them to participate regularly | participation fees and related expenses can discourage people with limited budgets, especially when they must also pay for transport or equipment | **学习工作忙→时间不足**；<br>**参与成本高→意愿降低**。 |

## 五、结尾替换表

**与原因素材选择同一主题，依次填入⑨—⑫。**

| 主题 | ⑨ 核心需求或价值：接在 importance of 后 | ⑩ 行动主体：接在 for 后 | ⑪ 具体措施：接在 to 后，动词原形开头 | ⑫ 预期效果：接在 so that 后，必须完整 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **线上购物** | convenient and affordable shopping services | online retailers | improve product quality, provide accurate information and offer reliable delivery services | consumers can shop with greater confidence |
| **阅读学习** | accessible learning opportunities | schools and libraries | provide reliable resources and practical guidance | more people can benefit from continued learning |
| **体育健康** | convenient opportunities for exercise | local authorities | improve public sports facilities | more residents can stay physically active |
| **旅游休闲** | convenient and rewarding travel experiences | tourism service providers | improve visitor services | travellers can enjoy a more comfortable experience |
| **参与不足** | removing practical barriers to participation | organizers | offer flexible schedules and affordable options | more people can take part |

### 结尾组合示范：线上购物

Taken together, these findings highlight the importance of convenient and affordable shopping services. It would therefore be useful for online retailers to improve product quality, provide accurate information and offer reliable delivery services, so that consumers can shop with greater confidence.

## 六、完整代入范文

**约151词。以下数据为演示自拟，不是真题数据。**

情境：2015—2025年，偏好线上购物的消费者比例从25%升到60%，偏好实体店购物的比例从75%降到40%。

The bar chart illustrates changes in consumers’ preferred shopping methods between 2015 and 2025. What stands out is that the proportion of consumers who preferred online shopping rose from 25% to 60%. Over the same period, the figure for shopping in physical stores fell from 75% to 40%.

Several factors may help explain this pattern. One possible explanation is that online platforms allow consumers to compare a wide range of products and prices easily, helping them make better use of limited household budgets. In addition, home delivery saves shoppers the time and effort of visiting physical stores, making online shopping particularly attractive to working adults and others with busy schedules.

Taken together, these findings highlight the importance of convenient and affordable shopping services. It would therefore be useful for online retailers to improve product quality, provide accurate information and offer reliable delivery services, so that consumers can shop with greater confidence.
